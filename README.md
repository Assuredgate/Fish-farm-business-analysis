Fish Farm Business Analysis

Project Overview

This beginner Data Analytics project examines the financial performance of a fish farm and related agricultural products using Google Sheets.

Objectives

- Calculate revenue, costs, and profit.
- Calculate profit margins.
- Compare the profitability of different products.
- Present findings using charts and a dashboard.
- Develop recommendations based on the available data.

Tools Used

- Google Sheets
- Spreadsheet formulas
- Data analysis and visualisation

Dataset

The practice dataset contains three products:

- Catfish
- Snails
- Moringa

The dataset includes revenue, cost, profit, and profit-margin figures.

Key Findings

- Total revenue: ₦1,800,000.
- Total cost: ₦900,000.
- Total profit: ₦900,000.
- Overall profit margin: 50%.
- Snails generated the highest total profit at ₦350,000.
- Moringa had the highest profit margin at 62.5%.

Business Insight

The results show differences in revenue, profit, and profit margins across the three products. Before making expansion decisions, the business should also consider customer demand, production costs, available resources, production time, and market prices.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of spreadsheet formulas, business metrics, charts, and data interpretation to support business decision-making.
